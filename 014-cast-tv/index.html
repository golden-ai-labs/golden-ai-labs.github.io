TV Screen Mirroring

Bring your phone content to a larger display with screen mirroring, casting, media sharing, and convenient TV controls.

📡 Connect • Cast • Mirror

Connect your Android device to a compatible TV or wireless display over the same Wi-Fi network. Mirror your screen in real time, cast supported content, display your media, or use available remote TV controls.

📺

Real-Time Screen Mirroring

Mirror your phone screen to a compatible TV or wireless display in real time. Useful for presentations, app demonstrations, sharing information, or viewing your phone interface on a larger screen.

🌐

Cast Web Content

Open supported web content within TV Screen Mirroring and send compatible media to a connected TV. Availability and playback may depend on the website, media format, network, and receiving device.

📷

Camera Cast

Display a live view from your phone camera on a compatible screen. Useful for demonstrations, group viewing, or situations where others need to follow what your camera is showing.

🎞️

View Your Gallery on TV

Choose supported photos or videos from your device gallery and display them on a larger screen. Media access is requested only when required for the selected feature.

📋

Use the Whiteboard

Create notes, sketches, or simple visual explanations using the built-in whiteboard and display them on a connected screen. Great for learning, presentations, discussions, and explaining ideas.

📌

Remote TV Controls

Use available remote controls for a compatible TV after connecting your device. Supported buttons and functions may vary depending on the TV model, receiver, and connection method.

🖥️ Connect to a Compatible Display

1

Connect your phone and TV or wireless display to the same Wi-Fi network.

2

Enable the required screen mirroring or casting option on the receiving device.

3

Open TV Screen Mirroring and search for available devices.

4

Select a compatible TV or wireless display.

5

Choose Screen Mirroring, Cast Web, Camera Cast, Gallery, Whiteboard, or Remote TV.

6

Use the Stop or Disconnect option when the session is complete.

📡 Compatibility Information

Feature availability, connection quality, and response time depend on your Android version, phone model, TV or display capabilities, supported wireless protocols, and Wi-Fi stability.

Some devices may not support every function. Certain features may work differently depending on the receiving device and its software.

Protected or restricted content may not be available for screen mirroring or casting.

TV Screen Mirroring does not remove or bypass digital rights management, subscription requirements, copyright protection, or content-provider restrictions.
