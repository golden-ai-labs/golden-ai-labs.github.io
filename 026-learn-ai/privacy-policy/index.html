✨ Privacy Policy

AI Learn: English Learning

Effective date: August 27, 2026

AI Learn: English Learning is an AI-powered English learning application designed to help users practice vocabulary, speaking, reading, translation, lessons, and conversations with AI.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it may be processed when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process only the information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application.

📱
Device Information Device model, Android version, app version, language, region, and other technical information.
📊
Usage Information General interactions with the application and information about features used.
💬
Learning Content Text or other content that you voluntarily provide when using lessons, translation, or AI chat features.
🎙️
Speaking Practice Voice or speech input may be processed when you voluntarily use speaking-related features.

2. Information You Provide

Some features allow you to voluntarily provide information or content to the application.

AI Chat

When you use the AI chat feature, messages, questions, or other text that you submit may be processed by the AI service used to generate a response.

Translation

Text that you submit for translation may be processed in order to provide the requested translation.

Speaking Practice

If you use speaking practice features, microphone access may be requested. Audio or speech input may be processed to recognize or evaluate your spoken English and provide learning feedback.

AI processing: Content submitted to AI-powered features may be transmitted to the relevant AI service or processing infrastructure in order to provide the requested functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Provide English learning features and functionality.
  • Process AI conversations and generate responses.
  • Provide translation and language-learning assistance.
  • Support speaking practice and speech-related features.
  • Remember learning progress and application preferences.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • Analyze general usage trends and feature performance.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements.

4. Learning Progress and Preferences

The application may store information related to your learning activity, such as lesson progress, vocabulary activity, completed exercises, preferences, or similar information needed to provide the learning experience.

Depending on the implementation of a particular feature, some information may be stored locally on your device or processed by our service infrastructure.

This information may include:
