Your Privacy Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, you have certain rights regarding your personal data.

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Please note that in some situations, we may not be able to fulfill your request, such as when:

  • It is required or allowed by law;
  • It could negatively impact someone else's privacy;
  • It would harm our rights or property;

a) Withdrawing Consent

If we process your data based on your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. This will not affect the legality of any processing that occurred before you withdrew your consent.

b) Access and Correction

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you and request a copy. You may also ask us to correct or update any inaccurate or incomplete information.

c) Deletion

You can request that we delete your personal data, unless we need to keep it for reasons such as:

  • Security or fraud prevention;
  • Fixing errors or debugging;
  • Exercising freedom of expression;
  • Other internal uses consistent with your relationship with us.

d) Restrict Processing

You may ask us to temporarily limit how we use your data if:

  • You believe the data is inaccurate;
  • The processing is unlawful;
  • We no longer need the data, but you need it for legal purposes;
  • You have objected to processing and we are verifying your request.

e) Object to Processing

You may object to the processing of your personal data in certain situations. We may still process your data if permitted by law.

f) Data Portability

Where technically possible, you can request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format, or ask that we transfer it to another service.

g) Automated Decisions

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (including profiling) that significantly affect you, unless authorized under applicable law.

h) Filing a Complaint

If you believe we have not complied with data protection laws, you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
